Records

Employee details, caller names, venues, dates, times, problem and resolution notes, and onsite information are stored in your browser. Optional Drive backup and restore use your Google account's private app-data folder. Upload replaces rather than merges cloud records. Deleting local entries does not automatically delete cloud backups.

Optional automatic Gemini summaries

After you enable and confirm automatic summaries, opening Stats or changing its cycle can send problem and resolution text to Google through Firebase AI Logic when the summary is missing or stale. Dedicated caller and employee fields are not sent; free-text notes may contain personal details. A further request sends grouped problem summaries with anonymous group indices to summarise reasons for venue calls. Disable automatic summaries in Stats to stop new requests; an already submitted request cannot be recalled. Cached summaries avoid repeat requests for unchanged records.

Summaries, AI classifications, supporting excerpts and weekly summaries are stored alongside originals and included in JSON backups and Drive uploads. Original notes remain intact for editing and Excel export. AI classifications and summaries may be inaccurate and should be verified before consequential use.

Third parties

Firebase App Check and reCAPTCHA Enterprise process security signals to help prevent abuse. Google processing and retention are governed by the applicable services, configuration and terms. No Google Analytics is initialised by this integration. Google sign-in and the Excel library also load from third-party hosts.

Retention and choices

Local records remain until deleted or browser storage is cleared. Back up before clearing storage. Manage app data in Google Drive settings; revoking access alone may not delete backups. Automatic-summary consent is stored on this browser and is not synced between devices.
