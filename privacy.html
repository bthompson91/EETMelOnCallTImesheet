Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

The On Call Timesheet stores your call records in your browser on the device you use. If you connect Google Drive, it can also save a private backup in the app-specific data area of your own Google Drive.

Information stored

The app may store the following information locally and, if you enable Google Drive sync, in your private Google Drive app-data folder:

  • Call date, start and finish times
  • Caller name, venue, problem, and resolution
  • Whether a call was attended onsite and any onsite times
  • Your employee name and selected week-ending date

Google Drive access

If you choose to sign in with Google, the app requests access only to its own private Google Drive app-data folder using the drive.appdata permission. It does not request access to, read, change, or upload files in the normal files area of your Google Drive.

The synced file is named On_Call_Timesheet_Data.json and is intended only for backup and restoring your own timesheet between devices.

Sharing and third parties

The app does not sell, rent, or share your timesheet information with third parties. Your data is not sent to the developer or any analytics service.

Google may process sign-in and Drive requests under Google’s own privacy policy when you use Google sign-in.

Data retention and deletion

You can delete all entries from the Entries page. You can also remove the app’s Google connection and its Drive app data through your Google Account permissions, or delete the synced backup from the app’s connected Drive data.
