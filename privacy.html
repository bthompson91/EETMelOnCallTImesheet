Automatic Google Gemini processing

This app automatically uses Google Gemini to check entry wording, classify call reasons and prepare weekly handovers. Problem/Resolution notes, including names and other details typed in them, are sent to Google when processing is needed. Continuing to use the app means accepting this processing. Suggestions never change your notes without approval.

Gemini processing is built into this app and has no enable/disable checkbox. Processing details are provided here rather than in an in-app banner. If you do not want notes processed by Google, do not use this version.

Problem and Resolution text is sent to Google through Firebase AI Logic for language checking, primary call-reason classification and weekly handovers when results are missing or stale. Notes can contain names, health information and other personal details. Weekly generation also sends venue names and generated call summaries with supporting excerpts. Dedicated caller and employee fields are not sent by these requests. Existing entries may be checked automatically when the app opens; new or edited completed entries are checked in the background. Cached results reduce repeat requests.

Suggestions and exports

Language warnings are advisory, not factual or legal assessments. A warning does not rewrite your notes. You can view the suggestion, edit yourself or keep the original. Applying a suggestion changes the stored Problem and Resolution only after your approval. Export warns about flagged or unchecked entries and lets you return to review or export anyway. Pending or failed checks must not be treated as a clean result. AI may miss issues, flag acceptable wording, or suggest inaccurate changes.

Local records and backups

Employee details, caller names, venues, dates, times, original/current notes and onsite information are stored in your browser. AI results, suggestions and excerpts are stored alongside records and included in JSON backups and Drive uploads. Optional Drive backup and restore use your Google account private app-data folder. Upload replaces rather than merges cloud records. Deleting local records does not automatically delete cloud backups.

Third parties

Firebase App Check and reCAPTCHA Enterprise process security signals to prevent abuse. Google processing and retention depend on the applicable services, configuration and terms. This integration does not initialise Google Analytics. Google sign-in and the Excel library also load from third-party hosts.

Retention

Local records remain until deleted or browser storage is cleared. Back up before clearing storage. Manage app data in Google Drive settings; revoking access alone may not delete backups. Requests already submitted to Google cannot be recalled by closing the app.
